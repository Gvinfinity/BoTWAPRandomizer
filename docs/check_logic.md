# Check logic corrections (0.5.4)

The source of the corrections is `worlds/botw/static/check_logic.py`. APWorld
catalog loading, shrine/boss event construction and chest discovery/binding use
it. The checked-in catalogs contain the same effective base expressions so the
client's item-condition hints see the corrections too. `Rules.py` still adds
parent region, boarding, DLC stage and goal dependencies.

The graph in `logic_dependency_research.json` is generated by executing the real
APWorld's `generate_early`, `create_regions` and `set_rules` against Archipelago.
Each named node contains its `requires` expression: `all` is AND, `any` is OR,
`ref` is a named dependency; a counted item/category reference requires that
many owned copies, summed across category members. `count`/`of` is an explicit
sum of listed items. Inventory items
are distinguished from local event items. There are no anonymous gate nodes.
Options and starting inventory are recorded at the top. It is the default-option
world, not a particular seed's item placements or a proof of physical gameplay.

```sh
/path/to/Archipelago/.venv/bin/python tools/export_logic_graph.py \
  --archipelago /path/to/Archipelago
```

Pass `--options options.json` to override game options. The generator verifies
access predicates, serialized expressions and complete reachability/event sweeps
against actual Archipelago `CollectionState` before writing the file.

## Changed requirements

* The nine chest identities in `logic_mistakes.txt` now have explicit rules keyed
  by logical map and HashId. E1F9BB4B and 78F0EAF1 accept Bombs **or** Paraglider
  **or** either supported Revali's Gale item, as confirmed by the player.
* Hinox/Stalnox and Talus encounters no longer require AP Bombs or a Master Sword.
  Native melee weapons, mining weapons, bows and ammunition remain obtainable.
  Elemental Talus may be attacked at range; cooling/contact immunity is an
  alternative strategy, not an AP equipment requirement for every strategy.
  Stalnox night timing can be reached by waiting and is not an AP item.
* Molduga retains the Remote Bomb route and also accepts the native bow/bomb
  arrows supplied by Flight Range preparation. These are renewable or native
  supplies, not a count of received AP arrows.
* Twenty Guardian Scout shrines use their exterior/quest requirements instead
  of mandatory armor upgrades or combat-aid pillar runes. Cave-opening Bombs,
  Shoqa Tatone's Camera quest and Tutsuwa Nima's scale offering remain modeled.
* Other inherited shrine expressions drop recommended hearts, stamina and
  generic upgraded-armor gates. Climate predicates accept native supplies.
  Plateau peppers model level-one cold preparation before the Paraglider;
  Rito-region supplies model the stronger cold preparation used later.
* Bosh Kala, Voo Lota and Sha Warvo explicitly require the Paraglider. Voo Lota
  and other Cleared-dependent shrine rules use the local freed-Beast milestone,
  not merely receiving a randomized Cleared reward. Zuna Kai's climb no longer
  requires Revali's Gale.
* Boss combat no longer requires recommended AP armor or particular AP swords.
  Fireblight retains Bombs and Thunderblight retains Magnesis. Existing Beast
  interior and Controls requirements still apply. The Sanctum route models the
  undefeated Fire/Thunderblight phases via their runes or freed-Beast milestones.
  Dark Beast follows the local Calamity milestone; Zelda supplies the native
  Bow of Light. Goal-selected checks remain additional requirements.
* Towers do not lock entire overworld regions. Their own traversal rules still
  apply at the check. Local supplies are available without locking all of Eldin
  behind fireproof clothing.

Vanilla equipment, vendors, cooking, enemy drops and quest gifts must remain
available for these native-resource branches. These events model a player's
ability to obtain supplies; they do not write native quest flags or report AP
checks. The player must still perform quests, solve puzzles and defeat enemies.

## Evidence and limits

The nine chest requirements are player observations, with the two flight
alternatives clarified directly. The remaining changes distinguish hard
requirements from combat recommendations and available native alternatives:

* [Nintendo's Explorer's Guide](https://assets.nintendo.com/image/upload/v1675114089/Microsites/zelda-breath-of-the-wild/pdf/ExplorersGuide.pdf)
  describes Plateau peppers, cooking and the native Warm Doublet.
* [Hinox and Stalnox](https://zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Hinox) describes native weapons,
  eye attacks and the night cycle.
* [Talus strategies](https://zeldacentral.com/games/breath-of-the-wild/enemies-and-monsters/)
  describes mining weapons, ranged attacks and elemental variants.
* [Tests of Strength](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Test_of_Strength) identifies
  the Scout combat shrines; upgraded equipment is preparation, not an entry key.
* [Bosh Kala](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/breath-of-the-wild-walkthrough/shrine-locations/bosh-kala-shrine-guide/)
  and [Voo Lota](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Voo_Lota_Shrine) describe wind
  traversal using the Paraglider.
* [The final battle](https://zeldauniverse.net/guides/breath-of-the-wild/walkthrough/chapter-15-destroy-ganon/)
  describes the native Bow of Light;
  [the Sanctum gauntlet](https://gamewith.net/zelda-botw/article/show/35951) includes
  undefeated Blights before Calamity.

This is not a complete physical audit of every mapped chest, shrine puzzle or
alternate route. Unchanged rune/scale/Camera requirements still inherit Manual
route assumptions, and unannotated Plateau chests retain their previous rules.
An empty local expression means the model assumes an ordinary native route;
it does **not** establish that the location was physically verified. Unknown
shrine chest-to-HashId bindings remain excluded as before. Additional confirmed
requirements should be added by physical identity, rather than inferred from
chest material, reward or region alone.

Logical collection-state tests and successful seed fills do not verify the
reported native rune-grant, duplicate-delivery or Plateau-barrier regressions.
Those require separate hook/patch/gameplay work. Existing generated seeds retain
their original placements: regenerate the seed and its Graphic Pack after
installing the updated APWorld.
